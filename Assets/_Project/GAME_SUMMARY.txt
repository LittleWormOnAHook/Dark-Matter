Dark Matter: Genesis is a companion-driven survival RPG — a story-driven sci-fi
RPG with a base camp on Jupiter's moon Io in 2160. You play as **Kade**, leading humanity's push to
prove a future there—not just scrape by, but rebuild under brutal conditions.
You manage oxygen and environmental pressures (radiation, cold/heat, volcanism,
sulfur storms, alien life forms, and AI androids), gather and craft, fight on
the surface, and grow a base camp of up to twenty-two companions while a trio of
three skilled companions can be switched and run expeditions with the player.
You rescue procedural Neural Echoes, spend Aether Credits at vendors, and
operate major structures through in-world Building Control Panels. A Lite
Building system supports expanding your base camp.

Long-term progression ties to **Kairos**—an ancient Echo sealed in a dormant
machine—and Memory Cores that trigger Resonance Events: short world-changing
story beats that unlock lore, systems, and base camp growth. Pre-prologue UI
uses shell/probe labels; **Kade learns the name Kairos at prologue end**. The emotional arc
moves Kade from survivor to explorer, leader, and founder of humanity's first
permanent home beyond Earth.

Under the hood, Dark Matter: Genesis runs on The World Engine, a World Operating
System (WoOS) stack built for Unity 6 + HDRP:

  World → Simulation → Intelligence → Experience → Presentation → Player

Gameplay systems (combat, inventory, roster, weather, building) feed read-only
GameState and WorldState snapshots; Directors and Experience decide when storms
spike, radio traffic fires, or narrative beats land; Presentation (Communications,
HUD, audio) delivers what the world authorizes—without AI or UI reaching directly
into managers. The design goal is a world that feels like it keeps running whether
you're watching or not: sulfur storms and Resonance Supercells pause base work and
force shelter; other weather threatens expeditions and exterior ops; expedition
crews carry full exposure risk, and future Resonance Events will reshape Io while
your base camp simulates injury, healing, and production at scale.

Dark Matter (The World Engine) is the reusable framework. Dark Matter: Genesis is
its first shipped game—the Io story, AC economy, Echo roster, Lite Building base
camp, and sulfur-storm companion fantasy layered on top.
