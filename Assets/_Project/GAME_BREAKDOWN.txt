1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Dark Matter: Genesis is a third-person Io survival + light base-builder built in
Unity (GDD 5.0). Year 2160 on Jupiter's moon Io: lead up to 25 companions
(22 base camp + switchable expedition trio of 3), rescue Neural Echoes,
expand your base camp via the Lite Building system, and survive O₂ plus
radiation, thermal, volcanism, sulfur storms, alien life forms, and AI androids.

[Sep 30] The playable prototype streams Gaia terrain tiles with Io surface
texture sets; the designed Io biomes are not shipped. Do not treat placeholder
scene art as forest / Earth wilderness camp fiction.

Core gameplay loops (vision + prototype):

  - Manage survival vitals (health, energy, stamina, oxygen) and exposure
  - Gather resources; craft at stations; spend Aether Credits (AC) at vendors
  - Lite Building + Building Control Panels — expand base camp and modules
  - Fight enemies (including alien life + android threats); complete quests
  - Switch expedition trio companions; run roster / Echo rescue systems
  - Use Building Control Panels (E) for Overview | Companions | Production |
    Craft | Changes on major structures

Runtime: Dark Matter: Genesis on The World Engine (WoOS) — see GDD 5.0 PRODUCT IDENTITY

Main playable scene [Sep 30]:
  Assets/_Project/Scenes/Dark Matter Genesis v1.6.5.unity
  (main scene — the latest v1.6; EditorBuildSettings still lists v1.6 first;
  Pioneer.unity is still on disk)
Core project folder: Assets/_Project/
Authority: Assets/_Project/GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT_5.0.txt

Movement [Sep 30]: Invector-bridged third-person controller (GDD B1); older
ECM2 references remain in some player scripts.
UI uses a Shift sci-fi theme with a journal hub for most panels.

2. CONTROLS
================================================================================

  WASD          Move
  Mouse         Look / orbit camera
  Scroll        Camera zoom (optics) / map zoom
  Space         Jump
  Shift         Jog [Sep 30]; double-tap to sprint (+20%)
  Ctrl          Crouch

  E             Interact (pickups, stations, companions, enemy loot, recipes)
  I             Open inventory tab (journal)
  J             Toggle journal hub
  M             Toggle world map
  Escape        Pause menu / close panels

  1, 2          Primary / secondary weapon hotbar slots
  3–0           General hotbar slots
  N             Scanner tool (toolbar)
  B             Tap: binoculars tool (toolbar); Hold: build mode [Sep 30]

BUILD MODE [Sep 30] (see section 22)
  Hold B        Enter / exit build mode
  Wheel / arrows  Cycle pieces on the build hotbar
  Left Alt + wheel  Rotate piece (free pieces by yaw step, grid pieces 90°)
  Right Alt     Cycle finishes
  Hold LMB      Build piece (hold ring / build bar fills, then cost is paid)
  Hold RMB      Destroy piece (refunds cost)
  E             Open / close doors; generator fuel panel

  LMB           Melee attack (hold for power hit)
  RMB           Block (one-handed weapons) / optics zoom when using tools

Weapon draw: press the same hotbar key again to draw or holster a weapon.

4. SURVIVAL & DEATH
================================================================================

FOUR VITAL STATS (SurvivalStats.cs)
  - Health
  - Energy (combined survival need; replaces hunger/thirst)
  - Stamina (sprint resource; regenerates when not sprinting)
  - Oxygen (displayed as MM:SS; 40 min full, drains to empty in 10 min real time)
    [Sep 30] Design lock: oxygen is needed in most areas; in some areas the
    player's Rebreather mask comes off automatically (not in code yet; planned
    as script + VFX/audio only, no model work yet)

MECHANICS
  - Energy and oxygen drain passively over time
  - Sprinting drains stamina; stamina regenerates when not sprinting
  - When energy falls below a threshold (default 25%) or oxygen is depleted, health drains
  - Consumable items restore individual stats (ItemData fields)
  - Optional health regeneration (disabled by default in current setup)

DEATH
  - Player death pauses gameplay
  - Death popup with retry / end game options (UIManager)
  - [Sep 30] Death overlay: Bio Gel revive / Retry (stand up at the death
    spot) / End Game
  - Respawn restores position and vitals (PlayerDeathHandler)

6. COMBAT
================================================================================

PLAYER MELEE (MeleeCombatController.cs)
  - One-handed combo chain (5 attack animation states)
  - Two-handed attack set (4 animation states)
  - Power / critical hit: hold LMB ~2 seconds, release for heavy strike
  - Charge wind-up pose begins at ~1.5 seconds of holding
  - Block with RMB (one-handed weapons only; two-handers cannot block)
  - Weapon switch via dedicated input

HIT DETECTION
  - Primary: WeaponHitbox.cs — box collider on equipped weapon scans overlaps
    during swing window (~0.45s); hits follow weapon motion, not camera aim
  - Fallback: sphere overlap in front of player if no weapon visual exists
  - Camera raycasts removed as primary hit method (fixes orbit-camera misses)

WEAPON STATS (per ItemData)
  - Melee damage + random range
  - Critical damage multiplier
  - Attack range, cooldown
  - Gather power (can hit resource nodes with weapon)

COMBAT FEEDBACK

  - Weapon swing and hit sounds (GameAudioManager.cs)

      [Loot All]  Take everything

22. BUILDING MODE & BASE EQUIPMENT [Sep 30]
================================================================================

BUILD MODE (Scripts/Building — DMBuildingMode, DMBuildingPlacementController)
  - Hold B toggles build mode; tap B stays binoculars
  - UI Toolkit build hotbar (DMUiToolkitBuildingHotbar): 10 visible slots,
    side arrows or mouse wheel; up panel picks a style or a known building
  - 4 m lattice snap with edge, top, and outer-corner snapping
  - Left Alt + scroll rotates (free pieces by yaw step, grid pieces 90°)
  - Right Alt cycles finishes (was M before Sep 28)
  - Hold left-click to build: a center-screen hold ring (build bar) fills
    (DM_BuildingGhostProfile buildSeconds 0.5 s; code default 2 s), then the
    cost is paid and the piece appears with creation FX
  - Hold right-click destroys a placed piece and refunds it (0.5 s in profile)
  - Ghost (DMBuildingGhost + DM_BuildingGhostProfile) turns red when the
    piece cannot be paid for; cost comes from inventory or storage crates
  - Not built: ghost commit + per-tick drain + cancel refund + reverse
    dissolve (scope plan Slice 2)
  - Placed pieces save and load (DMBuildingSaveRuntime); scene cleaner tool

STYLES (Resources/Building/Styles; prefabs in Prefabs/Buildings/Library/)
  Stone     69 ids, paid with Rock          finishes stone_cut / stone_rough1 /
                                            stone_rough
  Iron      65 ids, paid with Iron Ore      finishes iron_plate / brushed / trim
  Silicate  65 ids, paid with Silicate Ore  finishes silicate_crystal / smooth /
                                            trim
  - Prefab counts: Stone 78, Iron 71, Silicate 68, Equipment 3, SurfaceItems 7
  - 8-piece mesh upgrade for all styles with multi-material slots, glass
    handling, and top-aligned foundations (DMBuildingMeshUpgrades)
  - Built tint material; creation effects profile
  - Piece families: foundation (incl. half-cell, triangle, steps), floor,
    ceiling, hatch, walls (full, half, quarter, window, wide window, slit,
    arch, vent, triangle), door frame, door, double door + frame, passage,
    railing, stairs (half, spiral), ramps, roofs (corner, inner, steep, ridge,
    rooftop), pillar, column, beam, brace, balcony, ladder, stairwell, 8 m gate
  - Steel and Amalgam tiers, skill gates, and in-place tier upgrade: not built

BASE EQUIPMENT
  - Force fields (DMForceField): ff_door, ff_door_double, ff_hatch_lid. Block
    bullets, enemies, and weather; open for the player and companions. Corner
    strips red with no power, green when powered
  - Generator (DMBaseGenerator, SI_generator): burns Plasma Fuel; E opens the
    UI Toolkit fuel panel; refuels from inventory, then crates in range; can
    be switched off
  - Base power (DMBasePower): 100 m power square from the first foundation;
    powers force fields, lights, and equipment; each load adds to fuel burn;
    powered lights dim when unpowered
  - Build Hub (DMBuildHub, SI_build_hub): build zone 50x50 m, +25 m per upgrade
    level, anchored where the hub was first placed. Cost placeholder: Iron
    Ore 15 + Metal Scrap 25
  - Storage crate (SI_storage_crate): Iron Ore 4 + Metal Scrap 10; saved per
    crate
  - Doors swing with E; double doors and gates split into leaves
  - Surface items: wall light, 3 ceiling lights, banner, poster, sign
  - Equipment can be moved after placement
