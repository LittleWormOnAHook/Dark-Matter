1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Dark Matter: Genesis is a third-person Io survival + light base-builder built in
Unity (GDD 5.0). Year 2160 on Jupiter's moon Io: lead up to 25 companions
(22 base camp + switchable expedition trio of 3), rescue Neural Echoes,
expand your colony camp via the Lite Building system, and survive O₂ plus
radiation, thermal, volcanism, sulfur storms, alien life forms, and AI androids.

The playable prototype is still flat terrain (Io biomes not shipped). Do not
treat placeholder scene art as forest / Earth wilderness camp fiction.

Core gameplay loops (vision + prototype):

  - Manage survival vitals (health, energy, stamina, oxygen) and exposure
  - Gather resources; craft at stations; spend Aether Credits (AC) at vendors
  - Lite Building + Building Control Panels — expand colony camp and modules
  - Fight enemies (including alien life + android threats); complete quests
  - Switch expedition trio companions; run roster / Echo rescue systems
  - Use Building Control Panels (E) for Overview | Companions | Production |
    Craft | Changes on major structures

Runtime: Dark Matter: Genesis on The World Engine (WoOS) — see GDD 5.0 PRODUCT IDENTITY

Main playable scene: Assets/_Project/Scenes/Pioneer.unity
Core project folder: Assets/_Project/
Authority: Assets/_Project/GAME_DESIGN_DOCUMENT_5.0.txt

Movement is powered by ECM2 (third-person character controller).
UI uses a Shift sci-fi theme with a journal hub for most panels.

4. SURVIVAL & DEATH
================================================================================

FOUR VITAL STATS (SurvivalStats.cs)
  - Health
  - Energy (combined survival need; replaces hunger/thirst)
  - Stamina (sprint resource; regenerates when not sprinting)
  - Oxygen (displayed as MM:SS; 40 min full, drains to empty in 10 min real time)

MECHANICS
  - Energy and oxygen drain passively over time
  - Sprinting drains stamina; stamina regenerates when not sprinting
  - When energy falls below a threshold (default 25%) or oxygen is depleted, health drains
  - Consumable items restore individual stats (ItemData fields)
  - Optional health regeneration (disabled by default in current setup)

DEATH
  - Player death pauses gameplay
  - Death popup with retry / end game options (UIManager)
  - Respawn restores position and vitals (PlayerDeathHandler)

6. COMBAT
================================================================================

PLAYER MELEE (MeleeCombatController.cs)
  - One-handed combo chain (5 attack animation states)
  - Two-handed attack set (4 animation states)
  - Power / critical hit: hold LMB ~2 seconds, release for heavy strike
  - Charge wind-up pose begins at ~1.5 seconds of holding
  - Block with RMB (one-handed weapons only; two-handers cannot block)
  - Weapon switch via dedicated input

HIT DETECTION
  - Primary: WeaponHitbox.cs — box collider on equipped weapon scans overlaps
    during swing window (~0.45s); hits follow weapon motion, not camera aim
  - Fallback: sphere overlap in front of player if no weapon visual exists
  - Camera raycasts removed as primary hit method (fixes orbit-camera misses)

WEAPON STATS (per ItemData)
  - Melee damage + random range
  - Critical damage multiplier
  - Attack range, cooldown
  - Gather power (can hit resource nodes with weapon)

COMBAT FEEDBACK
  - Floating damage numbers (CombatUiSpawner.cs)
  - Blood splatter VFX (CombatHitVfx.cs, FX_Blood_Splatter prefab)
  - Weapon swing and hit sounds (GameAudioManager.cs)
  - Enemy noise events alert nearby AI (EnemyNoiseEvents.cs)

TRAINING DUMMY
  - Practice target with high HP (TrainingDummy.cs)
  - Floating health bar and damage numbers (DummyCombatUI.cs)
  - Prefab: Prefabs/Combat/TrainingDummy.prefab
  - Placed in Pioneer.unity scene

7. ENEMIES & LOOT

ENEMY TYPES

  - Definitions: Assets/_Project/Data/Enemies/*.asset (EnemyDefinition.cs)

ENEMY AI (EnemyAiController.cs)
  - Behavior presets: Aggressive Hunter, Guard, Patrol Investigator, Ambush
  - Movement modes: Stationary, Idle, Wander, Patrol
  - States: Idle, Wander, Patrol, Investigate noise, Return home, Chase,
    Attack, Search for lost target
  - Senses: vision (range + FOV), hearing, proximity (EnemySenses.cs)
  - Responds to player noise from combat and movement

ENEMY COMBAT
  - Melee attacks with windup, cooldown, and damage to player SurvivalStats
  - Floating health bar above enemy (EnemyHealthBarPresenter.cs)
  - Death animation and AI/combat shutdown on death

RESPAWN
  - Enemies can respawn at spawn point after configurable delay
  - Loot phase pauses respawn until loot is taken or loot timer expires (20s)

ENEMY LOOT (EnemyLootable.cs)
  - Enabled on enemy prefabs via EnemyDefinition loot settings
  - On death: rolls AC coins (min/max) and random items from loot pool
  - Empty loot pool falls back to item registry (excluding quest items and
    melee weapons from random rolls)
  - Press E near corpse opens loot dialog (EnemyLootDialogUI.cs):
      [Loot]      Take one reward entry
      [Loot All]  Take everything
      [Close]     Close dialog; corpse remains lootable until empty or timer
  - Respawn resumes after all loot taken or loot delay expires

EDITOR
  - Enemy Prefab Creator (Tools > Dark Matter: Genesis > Combat)
  - Update All Enemy Prefabs And Scene (EnemyLootSetupUtility.cs)

RESOURCE NODES (ResourceNode.cs)

  - Yield items added to inventory via ResourceGatherer

  - Press E to collect into inventory
  - Optional respawn timer (20–90 seconds)

BUILDING SYSTEM (GDD 5.0 — planned stack; prototype partial)

maintain over time. Major structures use Building Control Panels (below).

core structures / grids). Module roles include:

  Generators       Power generation for the base
  Power grids      Distribute and route power between modules / buildings
  Auto gather      Automated resource gathering nodes and harvesters
  Logistics        Move materials between storage, craft queues, and modules
  Communications   Comms / uplink / scan support (ties toward Probe Uplink)
  Defense          Turrets, barriers, base defense modules
  Mining           Mining drills / extractors (ties toward Geothermal Harvester)

  Command Center, Purification Hub, Geothermal Harvester, Echo Reclamation

12. AETHER CREDITS (AC)
================================================================================

Aether Credits (AC) is the only in-game currency (GDD 5.0 lock).
Starter grant on new game: 5000 AC → choose 1 starter Skilled Companion.
Legacy save JSON fields (piBalance, piWalletBalance) migrate into AC on load only.

DISPLAY
  - HUD balance: `UIManager.currencyHudText` → "AC: {amount}"

SOURCES
  - New-game starter grant (5000 AC for skilled companion choice)
  - Quest completion rewards
  - Enemy loot rolls (AC drop range on corpses)
  - AC-infused item pickups in the world (`ItemData.isAcInfused` / `acValue`)

FEEDBACK
  - Animated reward popup on gain (`AcRewardPopup` prefab)

PERSISTENCE
  - `GameSaveData.aetherCredits` (primary); legacy pi* fields folded on load
