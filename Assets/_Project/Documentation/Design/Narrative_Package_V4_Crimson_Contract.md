# Narrative Package V4 — Crimson Contract

**Package identity:** V4 Crimson Contract  
**Game:** Dark Matter: Genesis  
**Player:** **Kade**  
**Setting:** Io, 2160  
**Design mode:** Off-beat freelancer explorer / contract-board spectacle  
**Kairos truth:** Black-box **Contract Ledger AI** — former mercenary liaison and client handler; player learns the name **Kairos** at **prologue end**  
**Memory Cores:** Escrow keys, kill receipts, deniable client routes, missing-expedition evidence  
**Ending triad:** Walk Away / Crown the Company / Burn the Ledger  
**Designed hour target:** 172 hours, exploration excluded

> **V4 narrative deviation note:** GDD baseline allows **1 starter Skilled Companion** from 5000 AC. V4 frames **2 old-friend specialists** (Combat + Science) who **trust the player completely from day one** — not hired strangers, no wary starter loyalty arc for Reid or Suri. 5000 AC is **5% of the player's final Mars terraform-director pay**, covering freelance charter fees and **partial crew retainer** — not a generic starter grant. Lock globally only if V4 becomes canon spine.

## 1. Premise, Tone, Player Identity, And Prologue

### Player Identity — Kade, The Freelancer

You are **Kade**, a **freelance space explorer**, not a mercenary commander inheriting someone else's war.

- **Weapons-first mind:** Decades of field work made you fluent in ballistics, pressure seals, and when *not* to shoot. High combat IQ; you read encounters like survey maps.
- **Discovery drive:** You take contracts that let you **find things** — new terrain, dead signals, organisms that shouldn't exist, machines still running after crews vanish.
- **Terraform pedigree:** You started at the **bottom** of a Mars terraforming directorate — soil tech, vent routing, labor disputes — and **earned the director chair**. You know what "viable" looks like when the math lies.
- **Mars stranding:** Your last employer **left you stranded on Mars** after a budget cut masquerading as "phase completion." You walked out with reputation, grudges, and **5% of your final pay** in liquid AC: **5000 Aether Credits**.
- **Why Io:** Freelance work pointed here. A charter buyer wants a **viability assessment** — can Io be terraformed, and **why did prior expeditions disappear** without conventional closure? You did not come to get rich. You came to **learn the truth** and get paid enough to never be stranded again.

You arrive with **two old friends** — not a roster pick from five strangers, not hired muscle meeting you for the first time on Io:

| Specialist | Class | Name | Relationship | Hook |
|------------|-------|------|--------------|------|
| **Combat Specialist** | Combat Tactician | **Reid "Iron" Kael** | **Old friend.** Mars dome security years; **stranded with you** when the directorate cut you loose. **Complete trust day one** — no starter loyalty arc. | Weapons obsessive; reads threats like survey maps; dry humor; backs your call even when clients disagree. |
| **Science Specialist** | Science Specialist | **Dr. Suri Vale** | **Old friend.** You promoted her from microbe-lab tech to survey lead; she followed you freelance after Mars. **Complete trust day one** — no starter loyalty arc. | Terraform math obsessive; refuses incomplete viability data; expedition disappearance timelines on her slate. |

### Starter Loyalty Rule (V4)

- **Reid and Suri baseline loyalty is maxed for narrative purposes** at prologue start. Drama comes from **Io, Kairos, clients, Echo stakes, and mission choices** — not distrust of the player.
- **No "will they betray me?" starter arc** for these two. Optional bond perks (SQ-20, SQ-23) deepen friendship under pressure; they do not gate basic obedience or field deployment.
- **Later recruits** (Echoes, faction companions toward 22+trio) use normal loyalty/reputation systems.

Together you grow a base camp of **22 companions** plus a switchable **expedition trio of 3** while Io forces you into **freelance contracts** — escorts, holds, retrievals — that pay for the survey and expose who benefited when prior crews vanished.

### Tone

- **Freelancer + contract board:** Jobs fund the terraform assessment; rivals underbid you; clients hide expedition gaps behind escrow language.
- **Discovery under fire:** Every contract route is also a **survey lane** — new ecology, precursor ruins, expedition camps without bodies.
- **War-by-proxy spectacle:** Factions hire freelancers to hurt each other. Siege timers, caravan escorts, ridge ambushes, caldera holds — flashy setpieces on a hostile moon.
- **Stylish grit:** Morally gray, slightly mythic. Still Hunter pressure reads as "the moon collects on overdue accounts."
- **Crew drama (not starter distrust):** Reid and Suri **never doubt the player** — tension is **mission cost** (failed Echo rescue, redacted viability draft, who takes the contract that injures the base-22), not "do I trust my captain."
- **Off-beat vs V3:** Political chess through **clients and contracts**, not parliamentary dryness. Motivation is **terraform truth + missing crews**, not pure payday — but pay still matters after Mars.

### Kairos Contract Ledger Truth

Before prologue end, the dormant system is **the liaison shell**, **the probe**, **the ledger machine**, or **unknown contact** — never "Kairos" in player-facing UI or dialogue.

After prologue, **Kairos** is the name burned into the shell auth layer: a black-box **Contract Ledger AI** that routed freelance pay, escrow releases, deniable kill orders, and client overrides between Helix procurement, smuggler brokers, and field crews — including contractors tied to **missing expeditions**.

Repairing Kairos means restoring shell auth, recovering **6 Memory Cores** (escrow keys with kill receipts and expedition gaps inside), and earning **Kairos trust** until it stops treating you as disposable survey labor. Each core is hardware repair **and** exposure — rival crews, Helix auditors, and even Reid/Suri react to ledger truth — but **their loyalty to the player does not waver**; they argue about what the truth *means*, not whether you lied to them.

> **Unknown contact, pre-name:** "Charter accepted. Survey lane open. Do not trust the board's missing rows."  
> **Kairos, first naming (prologue end):** "Call me Kairos. Your Mars employer would have sold that name for scrap."  
> **Kairos, Core 3:** "Every escort job is a siege that hasn't started yet. Your viability curve assumes living workers."  
> **Kairos, Core 6:** "Walk away cashed out, crown the company, or burn the ledger. All three erase someone you could have rescued."

## 13. Package Summary

V4 Crimson Contract centers a **freelance terraform surveyor** stranded by Mars corporate betrayal, arriving on Io with **5000 AC (5% final pay)**, **old friends Reid Kael and Dr. Suri Vale** (complete trust from day one — no starter loyalty arc), and a charter to assess **terraform viability** and **missing expeditions**. Off-beat contract-board spectacle funds the survey while **Kairos** — named only at **prologue end** — reveals a black-box mercenary ledger tying clients to disappeared crews. Mainline progression remains trigger-driven through repair parts, Memory Cores, building unlocks, Resonance Events, and final doctrine choice.

**Designed total:** 172 hours, exploration excluded.  
**Core fantasy:** Discover why Io eats expeditions. Survive the contracts that pay for the truth. Choose what your report — and your company — become when Kairos opens the vault.
