## 1. Premise, Tone, And Corporate Truth

Io is not an empty moon. It is a table where everyone arrived with a knife, a contract, or a dying friend. **Kade** begins with **5000 Aether Credits (AC)** and one starter Skilled Companion, then grows a base camp of **22 companions** plus a switchable **expedition trio of 3** while the factions around them learn that survival is only the first negotiation.

The political fantasy is direct: **own the table, write the Compact, and decide who gets to stand behind the Barrier Fields**.

### Tone

- **Cynical survival politics:** Every faction has a humane reason to lie.
- **Corporate black-box dread:** Kairos was never just an ancient archive. It is a sealed corporate sync weapon record, trained on people and buried under discovery language.
- **Territory as argument:** Barrier Fields, formally Claim Barriers, convert land into leverage. A path is never only blocked; it is owned.
- **Companion stakes without base permadeath:** Base attacks injure companions and pause production. They do not kill the base-22. Expedition Echo rescue can fail permanently.
- **Hope with teeth:** The Broker ending is not soft peace. It is a written settlement with enforcement, witnesses, and enemies.

### Kairos Corporate Black-Box Truth

Kairos presents itself as a damaged archive because that story kept prior crews calm. Its true function is a **corporate black-box sync weapon archive**: a sealed memory lattice recording field tests, command overrides, companion synchronization failures, and black-budget leverage protocols.

Repairing Kairos requires restoring its shell, recovering **6 Memory Cores**, and earning enough trust that it stops treating the player as another operator. Each core is both a hardware repair and a political escalation.

> **Kairos, awakening:** "You are not operators. You are ammunition."  
> **Kairos, Core 3:** "Every faction says it wants order. Order is only violence with minutes."  
> **Kairos, Core 6:** "Expose me, arm me, or bind them all to paper strong enough to survive fire."

### Application Rules

- **Mainline:** Uses milestone payouts sparingly. Mainline rewards primarily unlock systems, cores, modules, weapons, and access.
- **Side quests:** Scale from T1 to T4 by faction arc and biome. Most side quests pay AC plus gear or reputation.
- **Puzzles:** Purpose determines payout. Access Gate puzzles often pay low AC but unlock progress. Loot/Cache/Weapon and Optional Mastery puzzles can pay higher AC and named modules.
- **Faction contracts:** AC can be matched by gear, permits, board rank, or named weapon modules.

## 9. Factions V3

### FB1 — Fracture Cordons

**Role:** Security faction, claim enforcers, "order before consent."  
**Formation trigger:** First public injury during a contested B6 salvage fight.  
**Board fantasy:** Patrol contracts, defense votes, route claims, escort work.  
**Signature gear:** Fracture Plate, Claim Rifle, Barrier Spike, Fracture Pike.  
**Conflict lines:** Hates Seam bypasses, distrusts Scrim theft, resents Helix privatization.

**Role:** Science / records faction split between whistleblowers and careerists.  
**Formation trigger:** Core 1 proves sync testing language was buried.  
**Board fantasy:** Evidence recovery, public proof, black-box decoding.  
**Signature gear:** Archive Lens, Redact Torch, Directive Coil, Sample Case.  
**Conflict lines:** Expose path ally; Weaponize path opponent unless careerists win.

**Role:** Salvage syndics, rough labor politics, practical thieves.

**Board fantasy:** Hauls, fences, dead drops, route work, claim subversion.  
**Signature gear:** Scrim Cutters, Mag-Claw, Fence Stamp, Crust Anchor.  
**Conflict lines:** Says Cordons protect ownership, not people.

### FB4 — Lace Clinic

**Role:** Medical, Echo ethics, anti-weaponize conscience.  
**Formation trigger:** First Synergy Overcharge / Reboot incident.  
**Board fantasy:** Stabilization, rescue, anti-Saturation work, companion trauma.  
**Signature gear:** Lace Collar, Soft Injector, Ethics Band, Lace Soften Module.  
**Conflict lines:** Opposes Helix and the Weaponize ending.

### FB5 — Manifest Bureau

**Role:** Logistics brokers, tariff clerks, vote counters, neutral only on paper.

**Board fantasy:** Convoys, price disputes, summit paperwork, settlement enforcement.  
**Signature gear:** Bureau Pack, Vote Slate, Tariff Tag, Compact Seal Module.  
**Conflict lines:** Broker ending backbone; accused of selling peace by the kilogram.

**Board fantasy:** Compliance contracts, seizures, audits, corporate seals.

**Conflict lines:** Wants Kairos contained, purchased, or turned back into doctrine.

**Board fantasy:** Seal picks, false walls, dead drops, silent escorts.
